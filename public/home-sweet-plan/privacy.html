Privacy Policy

Home Sweet Plan by Fimly Pooly Game Studios · Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

No accounts, no names, no emails. Your progress lives on your phone. Ads and purchases are handled by Google or Apple.

What the game stores

Your progress (houses, stars, coins, upgrades and settings) is saved on your device. The game also creates a random player ID so a support request or a cloud save can be matched to your progress. It isn't linked to your name, email or contacts, and we don't ask for your location.

Cloud save

If you're signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or iCloud (iPhone), a copy of your progress is saved to that account so a new phone can pick up where you left off. Google or Apple stores it under their own privacy policies; we can't see your account details.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and general device information (such as model, language and approximate region from your IP address) to show, measure and limit ads. Where the law requires it, you'll be asked for consent first, and on iPhone you'll be asked before any tracking. You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone's settings, or buy "Remove ads".

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation of what was bought.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase to collect anonymous gameplay statistics (for example, which houses are finished and which items are bought) and crash reports, so we can fix problems and improve the game. These are tied to the random player ID and device information, never to your name or email.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules a daily reminder on your phone. No server is involved, and you can turn reminders off in Settings at any time.

Children

The game is meant for players 13 and older. It isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset progress erases your save on the phone. Uninstalling the app removes it completely. To delete a cloud save, remove the game's data from Google Play Games or iCloud, or email us with your player ID (shown in Settings) and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the policy in the game and on fimlypooly.com.
